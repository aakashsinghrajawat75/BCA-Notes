## ⭐ Tips for Exam Preparation

- ✅ Start with **Topper Notes** for each subject — they're concise and exam-focused
- ✅ Solve **Previous Year Question Papers** (`3rd Year QPs.pdf`)
- ✅ Use **Unit-wise notes** for deep understanding of individual topics
- ✅ Revise **assignments** — similar questions often appear in exams
- ✅ For Deep Learning, focus on `Backprop_and_Optimizers.pdf` — most asked topic
